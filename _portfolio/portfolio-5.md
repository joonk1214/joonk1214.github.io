Investigated recurring PECVD particle defects and optimized RPSC NF₃ flow to reduce post-clean defect excursions and improve yield stability.

**Key outcome**
- Identified the PECVD encapsulation layer as the primary defect source through FIB-SEM analysis
- Established post-clean residual byproducts as a key contributor to increased particle defects during the early RPSC counts
- Reduced post-clean particle spikes and improved defect stability through RPSC NF₃ flow optimization
